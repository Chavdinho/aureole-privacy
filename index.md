# Aureole privacy policy

*Last updated: 5 November 2026. Applies to Aureole from Google Play (package `app.aureole.play`), version 1.0.0 and later.*

**In short:** Aureole shows your notifications in a pill around your phone's camera. It reads them on your phone, uses them only to draw the pill, and keeps nothing it doesn't need. It has no internet access, so nothing it reads can leave your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

## What Aureole reads, and why

| Permission | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| **Notification access** | Reads notifications as they arrive (app, title, text, picture, buttons) and the music that's playing, to show them in the pill and to work their buttons (Answer, Reply, Pause and so on) when you tap them there. |
| **Accessibility service** | The only way Android lets an app draw over the status bar around the camera. Aureole uses it to draw the pill and to know which app is in front (its package name only), so it can hide over full-screen video and games and over apps you choose. It does **not** read what's on your screen: its setting to retrieve window content is off. Aureole explains this and asks you to accept before it opens Android's accessibility settings; if you decline, nothing is switched on. |
| **Post notifications** (optional) | Only for the sample notifications and matches in Aureole's Try it out screen. |
| **View network and Wi-Fi status** | Only to know when Data Saver, Wi-Fi, the hotspot or a VPN switches on or off, for a brief moment in the pill. It gives no internet access and reads no network names. |
| **Google Play billing** | Only to buy Aureole Pro and to restore it on a new phone. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's privacy policy; Aureole never sees your payment details and only learns whether Pro is unlocked. |
| **Apps you can open** | Shows each app's name and icon in the pill and in the list of apps you can switch the pill on or off for. |

Aureole does not ask for, and cannot get: internet access, your location, your contacts, your call log, your camera or microphone, or your files. Call back on a missed call opens your phone app with the number filled in; you place the call yourself.

## What Aureole keeps

- **Your settings**, on your phone only: your looks, which apps use the pill, which apps it hides over, which alerts to clear from the shade, apps you've muted for an hour (with the time the mute ends), the time a pause ends if you've paused Aureole, and whether Aureole Pro is unlocked. Snoozing hands the notification back to Android, which shows it again later.
- **A short memory while an event is live**, such as a match's score and its goals so far, or a parcel's stops left, delivery window and item name from the parcel app's alerts, held in memory and gone when the event ends (full time, delivered, the end of the day) or the app stops.
- **On the lock screen**, while your phone is locked with a PIN, pattern or password, the pill shows only which app a notification or message is from, never what it says, and offers no reply until you unlock.
- **Nothing else.** Notifications aren't stored, logged or copied anywhere.

## Sharing

Aureole shares nothing with anyone, because it has no internet access. Android's cloud backup is switched off for Aureole. If you move to a new phone by cable or tap-to-transfer, only your settings move with you; Aureole Pro is restored from Google Play.

## Your choices

You can turn off Aureole's notification access or accessibility service at any time in Android's Settings, switch the pill off for any app inside Aureole, or uninstall it, which deletes everything it kept.

## Children

Aureole isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
